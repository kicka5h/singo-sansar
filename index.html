Who We Are

Rooted in Sarlahi. Growing Together.

Singo Sansar — meaning "the whole world" in Nepali — was founded with a single belief: that every woman deserves the tools to build a secure and dignified life.

Operating in the Sarlahi district of Nepal's Madhesh Province, we work alongside local communities to deliver practical training, mentorship, and financial literacy to women who have been historically underserved.

What We Do

Programs That Create Real Change

Vocational Skills Training

Hands-on workshops in tailoring, handicrafts, food processing, and small-business operations.

Financial Literacy

Practical money management, savings habits, budgeting, and access to microfinance resources.

Community Mentorship

Peer circles and mentor networks connecting women across Sarlahi for shared support and growth.

Education Support

Literacy programs and scholarships that keep girls and women in school and open new career pathways.
